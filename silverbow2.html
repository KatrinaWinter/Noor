About Silverbow

Silverbow the Seeker is a level 80 Marksmanship Hunter of the Khadgar realm, known for her precision, patience, and steady aim. Fighting from a distance, she relies on skill and awareness rather than raw force—striking with accuracy when it matters most. Her name is inspired by Birgitte Silverbow from my favorite novel series, The Wheel of Time by Robert Jordan, reflecting both her role as an archer and a sense of legacy carried into the game world.
With a focus on control and consistency, her journey is shaped by discipline and adaptability. Whether tracking enemies or supporting allies from afar, Silverbow remains calm under pressure—enduring, adjusting, and delivering each shot with purpose.

Silverbow’s Friends

  • Noor the Hallowed

    Noor the Hallowed is a level 80 Elemental Shaman and one of Silverbow’s closest allies. Channeling the power of lightning and fire, she supports from a distance with controlled, elemental precision.

  • Neytire, Assistant Professor

    Neytire is a level 80 Protection Paladin on the Khadgar realm and one of Silverbow’s most dependable allies. With strong defenses and unwavering presence, she protects her companions on the front line absorbing damage and maintaining control in even the most intense encounters.
